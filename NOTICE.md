## Data sources

- [TCGdex](https://tcgdex.dev) — Pokémon set and card metadata.
- [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) — Magic: The Gathering card data.
- [pullrates.com](https://pullrates.com) and TCGplayer — published booster pull-rate studies
  used to model pack composition.
